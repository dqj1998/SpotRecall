# What's New — 1.5.5 (all 21 locales)

Neither the Chrome Web Store nor Edge Add-ons has a "What's new" field. Replace
the 1.5.3 paragraph (the last paragraph of each locale's description, after the
ContextWizard cross-promo paragraph) with the matching block below.

The paragraph deliberately names no browser: Edge Add-ons warns "Avoid
referencing other browsers in your extension description such as Chrome", so
the same text is used on both stores. "Browser-internal pages" covers
`chrome://` / `edge://` settings pages without naming a browser.

What else ships with 1.5.5 on the dashboard:

- **All 21 locales**: listing copy and screenshots unchanged. Only the package
  and the What's New paragraph change.
- **Behavior**: auto-close now prioritizes blank new-tab pages, duplicate tabs
  (one copy is always kept), and easily reopenable local pages — files, local
  development addresses, and browser-internal settings pages. Local pages are
  fully protected for their first hour open, then tidied first.

## en
What's new in 1.5.5: auto-close now tidies up the most expendable tabs first — blank new-tab pages, extra copies of a page you already have open (one copy is always kept), and local pages that are easy to reopen, such as files, local development addresses, and browser-internal settings pages. Local pages are only tidied after they have been open for an hour, so a page you just opened is not closed.
