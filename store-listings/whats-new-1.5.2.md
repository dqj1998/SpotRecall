# What's New — 1.5.2 (all 21 locales)

Neither the Chrome Web Store nor Edge Add-ons has a "What's new" field. Replace
the 1.5.1 paragraph (the last paragraph of each locale's description, after the
ContextWizard cross-promo paragraph) with the matching block below.

What else ships with 1.5.2 on the dashboard:

- **All 21 locales**: listing copy and screenshots unchanged. Only the package
  and the What's New paragraph change.

## en
What's new in 1.5.2: auto-close now keeps the tabs you were just using. Pages with little text, such as sign-in or confirmation steps, and pages SpotRecall cannot read, such as store pages, could be closed only minutes after you left them. They are now kept like any other recently used tab, and are tidied up first only after an hour without use.
