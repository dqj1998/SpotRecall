# What's New — 1.5.0 (en, ja)

Neither the Chrome Web Store nor Edge Add-ons has a "What's new" field. Append
the matching block as the last paragraph of each locale's description, replacing
the previous release's paragraph.

This release ships new listing copy for `en` and `ja` only, so only those two
locales are written here. The other 19 locales keep their 1.3.1 listing and
What's New paragraph until their copy is updated.

---

## en
Auto-close is now on for new installs. Right after install, SpotRecall indexes the full text of the tabs you already have open, then closes idle ones down to your keep limit (12 by default). Every closed tab shows up in the 48-hour list and stays searchable. If you already use SpotRecall, nothing changes; turn auto-close on from the toolbar popup whenever you want it.

---

## ja
新規インストールでは自動クローズが最初からオンになりました。インストール直後に、開いているタブの本文までインデックスしてから、使っていないタブを保持数（既定 12 枚）まで閉じます。閉じたタブは48時間の一覧に表示され、検索もできます。すでにお使いの方の設定は変わりません。自動クローズはツールバーのポップアップからいつでもオンにできます。
